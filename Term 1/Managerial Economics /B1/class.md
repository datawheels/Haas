# Block 1 — Managerial Economics: Intro to Decision Making

## Objectives
By the end of this block, you will be able to:
1. Appreciate the value of decision trees and how to use them
2. Acquire tools to model uncertainty in decision making
3. Understand the most important ways to determine economic costs for decision making
4. Map the relationship between willingness to pay and demand formation

## Class 2 — Decision Making Under Uncertainty and Economic Costs [9/5]

### Decision Making Under Uncertainty
Continuing decision trees for random outcomes: "nature" nodes, probabilities over states of nature, value of information, and pricing real options.

**Before Class**
- [HermalinLectureNotes_v6.pdf](HermalinLectureNotes_v6.pdf), pp. 9–26
- Samuelson & Marks [7th: Ch.12 to p.519, Ch.13 to p.544] [8th: Ch.12 to p.394, Ch.13 to p.418] [9th: Ch.13 to p.388, Ch.14 to p.406]
- Besanko & Braeutigam Ch. 15.4, Analyzing risky decisions [6e: 665–672; 5e: 633–639; 4e: 627–633]
- Read [Class 2/Merck Pharma and Covid Vaccines.pdf](Class%202/Merck%20Pharma%20and%20Covid%20Vaccines.pdf) (Merck-Covid case) and be ready to answer:
  1. What is the order of events and the decisions to be made? Draw the decision tree for the case.
  2. What judgement calls do you need to make in constructing the decision tree?
  3. How much would it be worth to know ahead of time if version B will succeed?
  4. Would the decision maker's degree of risk aversion possibly change their choices?
  - Our worked decision tree: [Class 2/Merck Decision Tree.md](Class%202/Merck%20Decision%20Tree.md) / [Class 2/Merck Decision Tree (Mermaid).md](Class%202/Merck%20Decision%20Tree%20%28Mermaid%29.md)
- Read [Class 2/Why Merck Is Betting Big on One Cancer Drug.pdf](Class%202/Why%20Merck%20Is%20Betting%20Big%20on%20One%20Cancer%20Drug.pdf) and think about:
  1. Why is Merck shifting R&D resources to its Keytruda drug?
  2. (Harder) For a risk-neutral monopoly with two drugs and R&D time split r₁ + r₂ = 1, success probabilities pᵢ(rᵢ) increasing in rᵢ — how does optimal R&D allocation depend on the shape of p₁(r₁) and p₂(r₂)?
  3. How would a risk-averse company allocate R&D differently than a risk-neutral one?
