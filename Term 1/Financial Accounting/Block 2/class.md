# Block 2 — Financial Accounting: Revenue and Costs, Working Capital, Assets

## Objectives
During Block 2, we will:
1. Learn the rules guiding the reporting of key items from the income statement and the balance sheet — revenue, cost of revenue, accounts receivable, inventory, long-term assets and others
2. Discuss the insights these numbers provide to the audience for the financial statements
3. Review the performance of several businesses related to the same metrics in today's economic and market environment
4. Debate potential strategic decisions using these metrics
