# Block 3 — Financial Accounting: Liabilities and Equity; Cash Flow Statement

## Objectives
During Block 3, we will:
1. Explain the difference between temporary capital (liabilities) and permanent capital (equity) and the tradeoffs between those; we will also attempt to determine the optimal ratio between these and discuss the consequences of poor choices
2. Learn how the cash flow statement is prepared and, more importantly, what insights this statement offers
