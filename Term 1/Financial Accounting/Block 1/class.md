# Block 1 — Financial Accounting: Introduction; Balance Sheet and Income Statement Basics

## Objectives
During Block 1, we will:
1. Clarify the course objectives, structure, and logistics
2. Explain the purpose of Financial Accounting and illustrate its value to business professionals, using the AI economy as an example
3. Learn about two of the main financial statements — the Balance Sheet and the Income Statement
