OK, so now, we've
discussed how to structure

your persuasive presentations.

And you feel good about
your key messages.

But since this is a live
presentation and not

just a document, we
should also consider

the emotions we want to
bring into our presentation.

Research shows that the
impact of our communication

is driven not just by the words,
but by how we communicate,

the voice and non-verbal
communication.

Let's consider what we
want our audience to feel.

What's our intention
and make sure

that this is congruent
with our impact.

For each slide or beat
of your presentation,

can you come up with a
clear emotional intention

that you have for your audience?

You could certainly
imagine a presentation

where it's all good
feelings all the way

through, happy, excited,
thrilled, pleased, good, etc.

But that might not be very
interesting, convincing,

or memorable for your audience.

Maybe, it's better to look
for a variety of feelings

throughout, like
a vertical takeoff

that surprises your
audience, anger at the data,

sadness at the impact, a story
that evokes feelings of hope,

and a sense of calm
and peace at the end,

a feeling of resolution.

This is how we come
up with vocal variety.

We get very clear about
what our intent is,

and then we look at
the different elements

in our communication that we can
adjust to create that impact.

Where can we bring clear
intention and conviction

into our non-verbal
communication?

It could be our facial
expressions, hand gestures,

eye contact, how we stand,
and where we move in the room.

In terms of our
voice, what can we

do with volume, pitch, tone
(like warmth or coolness),

speed, emphasis, silence,
even the use of pause?

So now, we practice our content,
bringing not just the words

but the emotions to life.

And once we know what
we're going to say

and how we want our audience
to feel when we say it,

we know that we'll be
interesting, convincing,

and memorable in our delivery.

And now we're ready to
work on that slide design!
