## Presentation format

- 4–5 minutes presenting + 2 minutes Q&A
- Must be **persuasive**, not informative — needs a specific, actionable call to action
- Written work must be done directly in bCourses via the Rumi app (no drafting elsewhere and pasting in)
- Generative AI **not allowed** for written work; allowed for slide design if documented

## Core framework (from the two videos)

1. **Know your why** — define a specific call to action in one sentence (Vid 1)
2. **Know your audience** — avoid generalizing by job title/demographics; think in terms of familiar/unfamiliar × interested/uninterested (Vid 1)
3. **Tune your message to your audience**, not the reverse (Nancy Duarte, *Resonate*) (Vid 1)
4. **Structure like a story** — vertical takeoff (hook) → middle (appeal to minds *and* hearts) → end (memorable call to action) (Vid 2)
5. **Make the call to action SMART-ish**: concrete, measurable, achievable, relevant — e.g. tying it to a specific daily trigger ("meditate 5 min via Headspace before your first morning call") rather than a vague ask (Vid 2)
6. **Prepare content on paper first** (3 columns: key message / emotional intention / images) before touching slide software (Vid 2)

## Slide design guidance (from `Ask.md`)

**Do:** high-resolution real photography (Unsplash), vector icons, own photos for personal stories, check contrast for room lighting, sparse/intentional templates, test in presentation mode.

**Avoid:** watermarked/low-res images, clip art, generic stock photography, crowded/text-heavy slides.
