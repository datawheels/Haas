## Slide 1 — Title / Hook
**"The Calendar Doesn't Lie"**

Think of someone whose results you genuinely want. Do you actually know what their week looks like — or just the outcome?

Most of us chase the result without ever studying the recurring weekly structure — the planning and the work — behind it.

---

## Slide 2 — Personal Story

I remember watching *The Social Network* back in 2010. I got so charged up — all I could talk about with my friends was starting something of our own. We'd sit around at lunch, brainstorming ideas, full of energy.

Slowly, that energy faded for most of the group — except for one friend and me. We were hell-bent on making it happen.

We finally landed on an idea, built a basic roadmap... and when it came to execution, we were all over the place. Six months in, we realized this took far more than we'd ever envisioned.

Looking back now, I know exactly what we were missing: a framework of planning, execution, and accountability.

---

## Slide 3 — The Math Reveal

It starts with a goal. But not just the goal — you need to know your *why*, because that's what drives conviction and determination. Then you take a hard look at your calendar, and you carve out the time.

Let's try it right now. We're all here for our EMBA — how many hours a week do you think that actually takes? I estimated 20 hours, on a non-class week.

A week is 168 hours.
Sleep takes 49. Work, including commute, 45. Exercise, 4. Showering and breakfast, 7. Dinner and cleanup, 11. Family, 10.
That's 126 hours already spoken for.

That leaves 42.

And before this program, those 42 hours were already spoken for too — friends, outings, projects, golf, hobbies, just relaxing.

---

## Slide 4 — Reactive vs. Deliberate

Now every one of us has to pull 20 hours out of that 42 — and we have to be strategic about what stays and what goes.

We have two options. We can be reactive — let life happen, react to deadlines, scramble to deliver. Or we can be deliberate — sit down every Sunday evening for 30 minutes, and plan the week ahead.

I know which one I'm choosing.

---

## Slide 5 — Call to Action

So let's start right now. Open your calendar. Block 30 minutes tomorrow evening. That's it — one block, one day.

Your calendar doesn't lie about what you value. Let's make sure it finally tells the truth.
